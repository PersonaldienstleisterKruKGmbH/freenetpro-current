How to redesign a website without throwing away its SEO value

A redesign changes more than colors. Protect rankings by mapping URLs, content, metadata, internal links, and redirects before launch.
Title:How to redesign a website without throwing away its SEO value
Reading time:8 min read

A strong website decision starts with understanding what affects the outcome. This guide is designed to help you scope the work, ask better questions, and avoid preventable surprises.

01

Inventory before redesign

Export indexable URLs, top landing pages, metadata, backlinks, conversions, and current keyword performance before changing the structure. This identifies the pages that carry real organic value.

02

Map every important URL

Each useful old URL needs a destination in the new structure. Keep strong URLs where possible and use direct 301 redirects when a page must move. Avoid redirect chains and sending unrelated pages to the homepage.

03

Preserve the reason a page ranked

A visually improved page can still lose visibility if essential copy, headings, internal links, or search intent disappear. Redesign the experience while preserving and strengthening the page's topical purpose.

04

Measure after launch

Check crawling, indexation, redirects, analytics, conversions, and performance immediately after release. Continue monitoring priority pages because migration issues can surface gradually as search engines recrawl the site.
