I.Quick answerService summary

What does Website Audit include?

The audit reviews the submitted URL and business context for technical, SEO, user-experience, conversion, and measurement issues. The result is a prioritized action plan, not an automated score or a promise of rankings.

Best for
Businesses that need to understand why a website is not earning trust, leads, or search visibility before committing to a redesign, SEO work, or a migration.
Starting point
Scope confirmed after initial review — the final scope follows discovery.
Typical focus
Technical and SEO findings · UX and conversion priorities · A practical action sequence

What the audit answers
A useful audit does not bury you in screenshots. It shows what is wrong, why it matters, and which improvement should happen first.
II.What's includedAudit scope

What you receive in the audit

A focused, evidence-led review of the website URL and business context, followed by findings in clear language and a prioritized action plan. It is not a generic automated report.

01

Technical foundation

Indexing, crawlability, redirects, Core Web Vitals, mobile issues, forms, security basics, and platform risks.

02

SEO opportunity

Search intent, titles, headings, internal links, local visibility, content gaps, schema, and pages that should earn traffic.

03

UX & conversion

First impressions, navigation, clarity, trust, calls to action, booking or checkout friction, and mobile journeys.

04

Prioritized action plan

A clear list of what to fix first, what can wait, expected impact, and which work requires development or content.
